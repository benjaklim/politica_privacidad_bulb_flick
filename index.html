Bulb Flick

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Bulb Flick (the "Game"), developed by Benjamin Klimczuk, handles your information. In short: the Game does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Information we collect

None. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no social features. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To work, the Game saves a few settings only on your device: which levels you have completed, music and sound volume, your language choice and which tutorial tips you have already seen. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Third-party services

The Game is made with the Unity engine. It does not include advertising or analytics services. The Unity engine may send basic, anonymous technical information about the device (for example model, operating system and screen size) to Unity Technologies to improve its engine. This information does not identify you. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Google Play, from which you download the Game, may collect information according to its own Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The Game does not request any special permissions on your device.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: benjaklimczuk@gmail.com
